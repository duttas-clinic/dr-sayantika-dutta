About

Dr. Sayantika Dutta brings world-class dermatological expertise to every consultation. With her MBBS foundation and a prestigious Fellowship from the British Association of Dermatology, she combines evidence-based medicine with a personalized approach to skin health.

Her clinic offers a serene environment where advanced technology meets compassionate care, ensuring the best outcomes for every patient. Specializing in both medical and aesthetic dermatology, Dr. Dutta is dedicated to helping you achieve your healthiest, most radiant skin.

  • MBBS - Medical Degree
  • Fellowship - British Association of Dermatology
  • Advanced Training in Laser & Aesthetic Procedures
  • Member of Indian Association of Dermatologists
